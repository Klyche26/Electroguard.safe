Electrical Safety

Simple checks can prevent unsafe use

Always inspect electrical tools and follow appropriate safety procedures before operation.

Before using a corded tool

  • ✓ Inspect the power cord for cuts, exposed conductors, or damaged insulation.
  • ✓ Check the plug and grounding connection for visible damage.
  • ✓ Keep the tool dry and use it in an appropriate environment.
  • ✓ Use the correct supply voltage and appropriate protective equipment.

Stop and inspect when you notice

  • ⚠ Frayed, cracked, melted, or exposed wiring.
  • ⚠ Burning smell, smoke, sparks, or unusual heat.
  • ⚠ Repeated tripping of a breaker or protective device.
  • ⚠ Unusual current behavior, noise, vibration, or intermittent operation.

About Electroguard.safe

Designed around safer decisions

Electroguard.safe presents a user-friendly interface for a proposed smart electrical tool condition checker with suspected fault classification. Its purpose is to make electrical condition information easier to understand before a tool is operated.

🛡️

Safety-focused

Built around the idea of checking selected electrical conditions before use.

🧠
